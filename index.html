What Ribbon does

Eight things, each one quiet. You write; Ribbon tidies up behind you.

Polish on Enter

Finish a paragraph and press Enter. A wave runs through it and fixes spelling, typos, punctuation and wrong words. Names stay exactly as you wrote them, and Undo puts everything back.

ill go up teh stairs→I’ll go up the stairs.

Alternate lines

Sometimes Ribbon thinks a sentence could be stronger. It leaves a suggestion in the margin. OK swaps it in. Skip keeps yours.

She was very tired→She was exhausted

AI score

Starts at 10 out of 10. Every suggested line you accept lowers it a little. Stay at 8 or above and the book is still yours. Typo fixes don’t count.

8.6healthy

Word alternatives

Double-click or right-click any word to see other words for it. Click one to swap it in. It even keeps the tense: walked becomes strolled.

walked→marched · paced · strolled

Your own AI, on your computer

Pick a model the way you pick a photo: one click to download, or choose any model file you already have. Nothing you write is sent anywhere. With no model at all, the built-in rules still do the fixing.

Manuscript-ready export

Export a PDF in standard manuscript format, the way agents and editors expect it: Courier 12 pt, double-spaced, one-inch margins, title and page numbers. Or export plain text.

PDF · Letter or A4.txt

Just enough formatting

Bold, italic, underline, chapter headings, centred lines and scene breaks. Nothing else to fiddle with.

Questions

Does my writing go to the cloud?

No. Ribbon runs the AI model on your own computer. The only time Ribbon goes online is to download a model you choose, or to check your license key once.

Do I need a powerful computer?

No. The built-in rules work on anything. The small one-click model runs well on a laptop with 8 GB of memory; the larger one likes 16 GB.

What happens after 3 pages on the free version?

You can still read, edit and export those pages. To keep writing, unlock full access once for $10.

Can I move to a new computer?

Yes. In Ribbon, open File → License → Release this computer, then paste your key on the new one. One key works on up to 3 computers.

Mac says the app can’t be opened.

Right-click Ribbon in Applications and choose Open, then confirm. You only need to do this once.
