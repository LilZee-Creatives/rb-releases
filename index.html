What Ribbon does

You write. The offline AI tidies up behind you.

Polish on Enter

Finish a paragraph and press Enter. A wave runs through it and the AI fixes spelling, typos, punctuation and wrong words. Names stay exactly as you wrote them, and Undo puts everything back.

ill go up teh stairs→I’ll go up the stairs.

Alternate lines

When a sentence could be stronger, the AI leaves a suggestion in the margin. OK swaps it in. Skip keeps yours.

She was very tired→She was exhausted

AI score

Starts at 10 out of 10. Every suggested line you accept lowers it a little. Stay at 8 or above and the book is still yours. Typo fixes don’t count.

8.6healthy

Word alternatives

Double-click or right-click any word to see other words for it. Click one to swap it in. It keeps the tense: walked becomes strolled.

walked→marched · paced · strolled

Questions

Does my writing go to the cloud?

No. Ribbon runs the AI model on your own computer. The only time Ribbon goes online is to download a model you choose, or to check your license key once.

Do I need a powerful computer?

No. The built-in rules work on anything. The small one-click model runs well on a laptop with 8 GB of memory; the larger one likes 16 GB.

What happens after 3 pages on the free version?

You can still read, edit and export those pages. To keep writing, unlock full access once for $10.

Can I move to a new computer?

Yes. In Ribbon, open File → License → Release this computer, then paste your key on the new one. One key works on up to 2 computers.

Does it need the internet?

Only to download Ribbon, to download a model once, and to activate your key. After that, Ribbon and its AI work completely offline.

Windows says “Windows protected your PC”.

That’s normal for new apps from independent developers. Click More info, then Run anyway. You only do this once.
